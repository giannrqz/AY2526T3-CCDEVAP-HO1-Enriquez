The Things I Keep Dear To My Heart



Playing Would Always Be One Of My Favorite Things To Do

I started playing when I was young. I got to play because of the consoles that our father brought us. This along with getting to watch YouTube introduced me to gaming. It showed me that there were a lot games that I could experience. Since then, I have played so many games over many genres. But, one of the most influential moments of gaming would be my introduction to MOBA games. One of these MOBA games would be Mobile Legends, which I play up until now. At first, it was just about characters fighting and the end goal would just be destroying the base. But, the more that I got into the game, the more it showed me that not only did it require skill, but it also required strategy.

This was when I discovered the MPL, which was a Mobile Legends tournament that had some of the best players in the world. This showed me the beauty of gaming, now it wasn't just about having fun, but it now showed me that there is that thrill in competing and showing that you are one of the best players. I watched the MPL and played casually, thinking that this is my kind of lane in gaming. No stress from people cheering, no studying how the enemy played, or that there was no risk, its either I win or I lose.

But, that all changed during the pandemic. ML tournaments started to pop-up left and right because people had no other things to do. This was when I joined my first tournament with my friends. We didn't win because there were a lot of people stronger than us, but that wasn't what I took to heart, instead I found the thrill of competing to be exhilirating. This would result to me playing in tournaments, not just with my friends, but also my cousin who also played with his friends. I played in tournaments because I chased the thrill. It was never about the prizes that came with it. It was about the recognition.

Fast forward to today, I don't really compete any more since its a hassle to go through when you're in college. But, gaming taught me a lot. It taught me about fun, strategy, resilience, and persistence. That feeling that you wanted to get better, it stayed with me.


Music Has Been An Integral Part Of My Life.

Just like how it was with gaming, I was introduced to music at a young age. Though, the difference between music and gaming is that no one introduced me to music. I discovered music on my own. Some of the earliest music that I listened to were some of Coldplay's music like 'The Scientist' and 'Viva La Vida'. But, one of my favorites when I was young came from a rock group called 'Europe' and my favorite song was 'Final Countdown'. I remember it so vividly, not because of the lyrics, but because of the guitar solo. I can still remember trying to do the guitar solo on a rock guitar plushie.

But, of course, as I grew older, my music taste also grew with me. Some of the most significant moments in my music listening was in 2018 when I first listened to IV of Spades, and in 2020 where I blew up in the number of genres that I listened to which ranged from Classical to Indie and to Rap.

2018, I first listened to IV of Spades. I got introduced to the group because of their Wish Bus performance of 'Mundo'. The song's guitar solo really captivated me, but it was the whole vibe of the song that made me love their music. As I grew to a big fan of them, the more that I listened to all of their songs. There was even a point where I just had their songs on repeat. But in 2020, they entered a hiatus. After listening to their final song before they went into hiatus, I moved away from them. 2020, I started listening to all kinds of genres. But, the most important one that I grew to love and still enjoy up until now are The Beatles. A band from the '60s. Discovering it for the first time was like a shock. Never would I have imagined a band that only lasted from less than 8 years would be able to introduce me to so much genres of music. From the love songs that they created in their early years, to their most famous in their final years. They are still one of my favorite bands of all time. They belong in a special place in my heart.

Up until now, I still get the joy of getting to discover new pieces of music. I don't enjoy music, I love it. It keeps me going. Music will always be an important piece of me.
